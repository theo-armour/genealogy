# Shirk Read Me

My grandmother Anne Bell Burrage was born a Shirk, in Philadelphia. This folder is about her side of the family: the Shirks through her father, and the Watkins and Fulton families of Little Rock, Arkansas, through her mother, Ida Watkins.

## The Shirks

* [James Whitehill Shirk](../Wiki/James-Whitehill-Shirk-1832-1873/James-Whitehill-Shirk-1832-1873.md) (1832–1873) ~ my 2x-great-grandfather. A Union Navy commander in the Civil War; the destroyer USS Shirk was named after him
* [James Carson Marshall Shirk](../Wiki/James-Cameron-Marshall-Shirk-1865-1918/James-Cameron-Marshall-Shirk-1865-1918.md) (1865–1918) ~ his son, my great-grandfather. An architect in Philadelphia. His middle name is given as Carson or Cameron
* [Anne Bell Burrage](../Wiki/Anne-Bell-Burrage-1890-1983/README-Anne-Bell-Burrage-1890-1983.md), born Shirk ~ his daughter, my grandmother. Her year of birth is uncertain

## The Watkins and Fulton families

Anne Bell's mother, Ida Watkins, was a daughter, or by another account a granddaughter, of Chief Justice George C. Watkins of Arkansas.

* [David Fulton](../Wiki/David-Fulton-1771-1843/David-Fulton-1771-1843.md) (1771–1843) ~ Mayor of Little Rock and Surveyor General for Arkansas
* [William S. Fulton](../Wiki/William-S-Fulton-1795-1844/William-S-Fulton-1795-1844.md) (1795–1844) ~ David's son. Governor of Arkansas Territory and one of the state's first US Senators. His daughter Sophia married George C. Watkins
