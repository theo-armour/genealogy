# Armour Read Me

My father's family, the Armours of Chicago and Princeton. His mother, Granny, was a Kudashev; her side is in the [Kudashev](../Kudashev/README-Kudashev.md) folder.

## The Armours

* [George Armour](../Wiki/George-Armour-1812-1881/George-Armour-1812-1881.md) (1812–1881) ~ my 2x-great-grandfather. Born in Scotland; grain elevators and the Chicago Board of Trade
* [George Allison Armour](../Wiki/George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936/George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936.md) (1856–1936) ~ his son, my great-grandfather. Married Harriette Cobb Foote
* [Allison Vincent Armour](../Wiki/Allison-Vincent-Armour-1863-1941/Allison-Vincent-Armour-1863-1941.md) (1863–1941) ~ George Allison's brother, my 2x-great-uncle. Explorer and yachtsman
* [Norman Armour Sr](../Wiki/Norman-Armour-Sr-1887-1982/Norman-Armour-Sr-1887-1982.md) (1887–1982) ~ my grandfather. Career diplomat
* [Barbara Armour Lowrie](../Wiki/Barbara-Armour-Lowrie-1889-1981/Barbara-Armour-Lowrie-1889-1981.md) (1889–1981) ~ his sister, my great-aunt
* [Walter Lowrie](../Wiki/Walter-Lowrie-1868-1959/Walter-Lowrie-1868-1959.md) (1868–1959) ~ her husband, my great-uncle. Translator of Kierkegaard
* [Norman Armour Jr](../Wiki/Norman-Armour-Jr-1920-1978/Norman-Armour-Jr-1920-1978.md) (1920–1978) ~ my father

## The Footes

* [Erastus Foote](../Wiki/Erastus-Foote-1777-1856/Erastus-Foote-1777-1856.md) (1777–1856) ~ my 3x-great-grandfather. The first Attorney General of Maine
