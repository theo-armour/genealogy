Sergio Pelà is an Italian creative figure primarily recognized for his involvement in the countercultural movements of Milan during the late 1970s. [1, 2] Key facts about him include:

  • The Indiani Metropolitani Movement: In 1977, he was part of the Indiani Metropolitani (Metropolitan Indians), a creative and satirical faction of the Italian left-wing youth movement. [1, 3]
  • Oask?! Magazine: Alongside Gloria Malatesta, Roberto di Reda, and Francesco Saglio, he co-founded and edited Oask?!, an underground counterculture fanzine published in Milan. The publication rejected traditional political dogmas in favor of creative, nihilistic expression and Dadaist irony. [1, 2, 4, 5]
  • Milan Youth Circles: He was an active contributor to alternative publications circulating through the Circoli del proletariato giovanile (Youth Proletariat Circles) of Milan. [2]
