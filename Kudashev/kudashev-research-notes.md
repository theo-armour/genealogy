## 2026-11-20 ~ How the Trepovs, Kudashevs and von Nieroths were linked, 1897–1917

A first search for connections between the three families in the two decades before the Revolution. No letter or diary has turned up yet that puts two of them in the same room. What is documented:

* **Two brothers married two sisters.** Maximilian von Nieroth married Anastasia Trepova. His brother, Count Mikhail (Mavriky) Evstafyevich Nirod (1852–1930), married her sister Sofia Fyodorovna Trepova (1853–1927), a maid of honour. Russian Wikipedia names Nikolai (1836–1888) and Maximilian (1846–1914) as Mikhail's full brothers. So Vera had cousins related to her on both sides
* **Those cousins died young in the Japanese war.** Alexei Mikhailovich Nirod (1882–1904), midshipman on the cruiser *Varyag*, was killed at Chemulpo. Georgy (1884–1905), midshipman on the cruiser *Svetlana*, was killed at Tsushima
* **Sergei Kudashev and Mikhail Nirod served in the same department.** Sergei was an official of the Main Directorate of Appanages, part of the Ministry of the Imperial Court. Mikhail Nirod was attached to the Main Directorate of Appanages from 1897, was assistant head of the Appanage Department from 1903, and in 1916 was Ober-Jägermeister and assistant to the Minister of the Court, Count Fredericks
* **A third Trepov sister married into the same ministry.** Yelizaveta Trepova's husband, General A. A. Mosolov, was head of the Court Chancellery from 1900 to 1916. Dmitri Trepov was Palace Commandant in 1905–1906. Maximilian was Jägermeister from 1898. Vera and Sofia were maids of honour
* **The women lent each other things.** Mosolov's memoir, on arranging an imperial visit: "we borrowed a sufficiency of silver from my sister-in-law, Countess Nirod". That is Anastasia or Sofia; he does not say which
* **The 1903 costume ball.** Vera and Sergei were there. The album also lists Vera's uncle Vladimir Trepov, as Stallmeister, and a maid of honour, Countess Elizaveta Mikhailovna Nirod ~ see "Possibly Related Images" below. Not confirmed that she was Mikhail and Sofia's daughter; Russian Wikipedia does not list a daughter of that name
* **Kiev and Volhynia.** Fyodor Trepov junior was Governor of Volhynia from 1896, Governor of Kiev 1898–1903 and Governor-General of Kiev, Podolia and Volhynia 1908–1914. In 1913 Maximilian was elected to the State Council by the Volhynia zemstvo. Maximilian is buried at the Vydubychi Monastery in Kiev, where Trepov senior is buried
* **The Horse Guards.** Fyodor Trepov junior, Dmitri Trepov, Mikhail Nirod and Vera's brother Fyodor all served in the Life Guards Horse Regiment

Two points for the person pages:

* Maximilian's biography says Mikhail "does not appear among Maximilian's siblings". He probably does, as "Moritz Nikolai Pontius": a German biographical entry (BBLD) lists Mikhail as "Nieroth, Moritz Nikolaus Pontus Gf. v. (1852–1930)". The match is on name and dates only ~ probable, not proven
* The Russian Wikipedia article on the Nirods lists a Fyodor Mikhailovich Nirod (1878–1913), Horse Guards, aide-de-camp to the Emperor in 1905. Vera's brother Fyodor Maximilianovich was also Horse Guards and aide-de-camp in 1905. Either they were cousins with parallel careers or a source has mixed the two up. The article on Mikhail himself does not list this son

Not found: any direct business dealing between Sergei Kudashev and a Trepov; anything on the Yusupov palace party; any correspondence.
