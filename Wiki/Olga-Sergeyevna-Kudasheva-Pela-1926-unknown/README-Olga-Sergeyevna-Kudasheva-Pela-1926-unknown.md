# Olga Sergeyevna Pelà, born Princess Kudasheva (1926–?)

**Olga Sergeyevna Pelà**, born **Princess Olga Sergeyevna Kudasheva** (1926 – date not recorded), was the only child of Prince [Sergei Vladimirovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933/README-Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933.md) and his second wife, [Olga Erikovna von Pistohlkors](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963/README-Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963.md). Her parents were living in Florence when she was born. She married [Achille Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Achille-Pela-1921-1986/README-Achille-Pela-1921-1986.md) in 1955. She was the mother of [Sergio Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergio-Pela-1957-/README-Sergio-Pela-1957-.md).[1][2][3] She was a half-sister of my grandmother, [Maria (Myra) Sergeyevna Kudasheva](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Maria-Sergeyevna-Kudasheva-1896-1990/README-Maria-Sergeyevna-Kudasheva-1896-1990.md), and so my father's half-aunt. She has died. The date isn't recorded here.

## Life

She was born in 1926, four years after her parents married in Florence, and in the year her father won Italian recognition of his princely title.[2][3] Her father was 62 or 63, and her mother 37 or 38. Through her father she had two grown half-siblings, Myra, then married to the American diplomat Norman Armour, and Sergei. Through her mother she had a half-brother, Count Alexander von Kreutz.[2]

Her father died in Florence in February 1933, when she was six or seven, and was buried in the Cimitero Evangelico agli Allori.[2] Her mother lived until 1963.[2]

Geni has a note that she was "the secretary of the American Consul in Rome".[1] In 1955 she married Achille Pelà. Geni places the marriage in the United States.[1] They had three children: Alexandra in 1956, Sergio in 1957 and Olga in 1964.[1]

An AI search summary in my notes says that she, like Achille and Rosanna Pelà, worked as a translator for the publisher Istituto Geografico De Agostini. No source has been seen for this.[4]

Her son Sergio holds family papers and photographs that came down from her father, including views of Villa Granduchessa in Florence.[3]
