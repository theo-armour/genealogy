## Additional Biographical & Family Details

- **Battle of Grand Gulf:** States that during the battle of Grand Gulf he engaged Confederate batteries for "five hours", an action his superiors called one of the "best stand-up fights" of the war (the README gives the 8:25 a.m. start time and 81 hits without the five-hour duration or this quote).
- **Descriptive characterization:** Refers to his river service as the "Western Waters" (the Mississippi, Tennessee, and Cumberland rivers), describes USS *Lexington* as a "timberclad" gunboat, and places him among the "brown-water navy" officers whose close-range support of Army ground forces helped secure Union control of the Mississippi River Valley.

## Alternate Names, Spellings & Locations

- **Mother's full name:** Listed in the Genealogy section as `Mary Ann Whitehill (married Shirk)` (the README gives `Mary Whitehill` / `Mary A Whitehill` and notes a separate 1840 burial of `Mary Ann Shirk` in Erie Cemetery).

## Discrepancies vs. README

- **Age at death:** `James-Whitehill-Shirk-1832-1873.md` notes he died "at the age of 40 (often cited as 41 in biographical contexts depending on the month)", whereas the README gives age 40 without noting the 41 variant.
- **Cause of death:** Attributes his death to "health complications resulting from his arduous wartime service", whereas the README cites the Washington *Evening Star* reporting that he died of pneumonia.
