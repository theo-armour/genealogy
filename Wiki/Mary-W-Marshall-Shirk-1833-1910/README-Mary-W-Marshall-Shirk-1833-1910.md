# Mary W. Marshall Shirk (1833–1910)

No portrait of her is held in this folder.

**Mary W. Marshall Shirk** (19 August 1833 – 8 August 1910) was the elder daughter of James C. Marshall, a lawyer and banker of Erie, Pennsylvania. She married [Commander James W. Shirk](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/James-Whitehill-Shirk-1832-1873/README-James-Whitehill-Shirk-1832-1873.md), US Navy, and was widowed in 1873. She raised their three children in Philadelphia, among them the architect [J. C. Marshall Shirk](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/James-Cameron-Marshall-Shirk-1865-1918/README-James-Cameron-Marshall-Shirk-1865-1918.md). She was Theo Armour's great-great-grandmother.[1]

## Life

She was the elder daughter of James C. Marshall of Erie and his wife Eliza Weatherbee. Her father, born in 1799, was a lawyer who settled in Erie in 1828, lived at Girard from 1830 to 1844, and from 1867 was president of the Marine National Bank of Erie.[1]

She married James W. Shirk, a naval officer of an Erie County family who commanded gunboats of the Mississippi Squadron in the Civil War. The date and place of the marriage have not been found.[1] Their son James C. Marshall Shirk was born in Philadelphia on 23 June 1865.[1][2]

Her husband died on 10 February 1873. In 1884 her father's sketch in the county history said: "Mary W. is the widow of Commander James W. Shirk, U. S. Navy (she has three children, all living in Philadelphia)". A MyHeritage index entry for the 1880 census names the children as David Marshall Shirk, J. Marshall Shirk and Fannie Shirk. The census page itself has not been seen.[1]

She died on 8 August 1910 and is buried in Erie Cemetery, in the same lot as her husband.[1]

## Open questions

* **Her dates.** The Erie Cemetery records, Geni and two trees give 19 August 1833 – 8 August 1910. MyHeritage gives 1840–1900. The cemetery dates are the better evidence.[1]
* **Date of the marriage.** The old profile page says "around 1861/1862", without a source.[1]
* **"A Southern Belle".** The Peeps page speaks of James Shirk marrying "a Southern Belle". Mary Marshall was born and raised in Erie County, Pennsylvania, so the phrase does not fit her.[1]
* **Where she died.** Erie or Philadelphia?
