# Alice Hathaway Haskell Burrage

🌳 [See in the GEDCOM Viewer](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947)

**Alice Hathaway Haskell** (6 October 1857, 1861 or 1862 – December 1947), known after her marriage as Mrs Albert C. Burrage, was the wife of the Boston lawyer and copper industrialist [Albert Cameron Burrage Sr](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Sr-1859-1931/README-Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Sr-1859-1931.md). Most sources say she was born in China. She lived in the Burrage houses at 314 Commonwealth Avenue, Boston, at Redlands, California, and at Manchester, Massachusetts, and she paid for Trinity Episcopal Church in Redlands.[1][2][3][4][5] Her birth year is uncertain (see Open questions). She was Theo Armour's maternal great-grandmother.[1][6]

## Family and early life

Alice Hathaway Haskell was the daughter of Francis Hathaway Haskell and Elizabeth (Russell) Haskell. Her husband named both parents in his Harvard class report and described the family as "of Roxbury, Mass." at the time of the marriage.[2] Her father was a son of [Elisha Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Elisha-Haskell-Jr-1795-1880/README-Elisha-Haskell-Jr-1795-1880.md) of New Bedford and his second wife, Alice Hathaway. The *Chronicles of the Haskell Family* (1943) lists their children as Thomas H., Frederick E., Alice H. (who married a Cunningham), Francis H. ("m Elizabeth Russell"), Charlotte G. and Caroline H., who married George Lee.[7] Caroline Watts Haskell and George Cabot Lee were the parents of Alice Hathaway Lee (1861–1884), the first wife of Theodore Roosevelt.[8] If these sources are right, Alice Haskell Burrage and Alice Lee Roosevelt were first cousins.

Most sources say Alice was born in China. Geni gives her birth as 6 October 1857 in Hong Kong.[9] A family tree that cites the 1900 United States census gives 6 October 1862 in China.[10] The Pacific Coast Architecture Database gives 1861 "in China".[3] Her father is said to have been a ship's master in the China trade; Geni carries the note "Master of ship Norseman" for him, and one family tree says he was lost overboard on 25 June 1868 on a voyage from Hong Kong to New York. *(Unsound: neither statement cites a source.)*[9][10]

Her mother, Elizabeth (Russell) Haskell, was a widow by 1899, when she bought the house at 7 Gloucester Street in Boston's Back Bay. She had lived before that at The Warren, at Warren and Regent Streets in Dudley Square, Roxbury. She died in September 1909.[11] Alice's sister Sarah A. Haskell and brother Francis Hathaway Haskell Jr (died March 1927) lived with their mother at 7 Gloucester Street.[11] The MyHeritage copy of Geni's record also names an Elizabeth Hathaway Haskell and a Robert Hathaway Haskell among her siblings.[12]

## Marriage

Alice Hathaway Haskell married Albert Cameron Burrage on 10 November 1885. He wrote in his class report the following year:

> November 10, 1885, I was married, by Rev. Percy Browne, at St. James (Episcopal) Church, St. James Street, Roxbury, Mass., to Alice Hathaway Haskell, daughter of Francis Hathaway Haskell and Elizabeth (Russell) Haskell, of Roxbury, Mass.[2]

Burrage, the son of the Napa vineyard owner [George Sanderson Burrage](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/George-Sanderson-Burrage-1823-1876/README-George-Sanderson-Burrage-1823-1876.md), was a member of the Harvard class of 1883 and had begun practising law in Boston on 1 October 1884.[2][13] They lived at first in Roxbury.[13] He later became counsel to the Brookline Gas Light Company, a member of the Boston Transit Commission, and in 1899 one of the organizers of the Amalgamated Copper Company.[13]

They had four children:[13]

* **Albert Cameron Burrage Jr**, born 30 August 1886.
* **Francis Hathaway Burrage**, born 14 March 1888; died 13 December 1911.
* **Russell Burrage**, born 17 December 1889; the Pacific Coast Architecture Database gives his death as 1957.[3]
* **Elizabeth Alice Burrage**, born 12 July 1895, later Mrs Harold L. Chalifoux.[3]

## 314 Commonwealth Avenue and the other houses

On 17 January 1899 Alice Burrage, rather than her husband, acquired the property at 314 Commonwealth Avenue on which the Burrage House was built. She also bought the vacant lot at 325 Newbury Street behind it in April 1899.[4] The house, designed by Charles Brigham in the French Renaissance château style, was built in 1899.[14] While it was being finished the family lived at the Hotel Somerset and, before that, at 140 Townsend. By the winter of 1900–1901 they had moved in, and the four children were raised there.[4]

The family also had a summer house, the Needles, at Hanson, Massachusetts, which burned on 27 May 1907.[15] They also kept a house called The Caravels at Cohasset.[4] One family tree cites the 1900 census for the family in Cohasset.[10] In 1921 they bought an estate at Manchester, Massachusetts, which they called Seahome.[4] The Boston Landmarks Commission names a further house, Orchidvale, at Beverly, where Albert grew orchids.[14]

## Redlands, California

In 1901 a winter house was built for Albert Burrage at Redlands, California. The Redlands Area Historical Society says the Burrages left Redlands after six years and did not return until 1916, and that the house was sold in 1924.[5] The society records that "Mrs. Alice Burrage played a prominent role in Redlands philanthropy", "her most notable gift being the funds for the Trinity Episcopal Church building".[5]

The church, also called the Burrage Memorial Church, was her gift. She gave a cheque for $20,000 to build it as a memorial to her mother-in-law, Mrs Aurelia Burrage. The contract went to the Taylor Brick Company of Redlands in May 1903, and the church, designed by Daniel F. Willard of Riverside, was finished in the spring of 1904.[16]

## Widowhood and death

Albert Cameron Burrage died at Seahome, West Manchester, in June 1931, with his wife at his side, according to the *New York Times* obituary as summarised by Wikipedia.[17] Alice Burrage went on living at 314 Commonwealth Avenue and at Manchester until her death in December 1947.[4][14] Geni and two family trees give the date as 21 December 1947 at Manchester; the Pacific Coast Architecture Database gives 22 December 1947.[3][9][10]

In her will she left Seahome to her daughter, Elizabeth Chalifoux, and gave her the right to buy 314 Commonwealth Avenue and the property at Beverly at a set price. Elizabeth bought 314 Commonwealth from her mother's estate on 19 May 1948 and sold it five days later.[4] The house then became doctors' offices.[14] No obituary or burial record has been found.

## Open questions

* **Year of birth.** Geni, MyHeritage, an Ancestry export and the profile page give 6 October **1857**.[1][9][12] The Pacific Coast Architecture Database and the name of this folder give **1861**.[3] A family tree citing the 1900 census, another Ancestry-derived tree and the profile page of her father give 6 October **1862**.[6][10][18] Geni also dates her parents' marriage to about 1860, which would not fit a birth in 1857.[9] The 1900 census entry and her death record have not been seen.
* **Place of birth.** Geni says Hong Kong; the 1900-census tree and the Pacific Coast Architecture Database say China.[3][9][10] One Ancestry-derived tree says Massachusetts.[18]
* **Date of death.** 21 December 1947 (Geni and two family trees) or 22 December 1947 (Pacific Coast Architecture Database).[3][9][10]
* **"Lillian".** Her father's profile page and one family tree call her "Alice Lillian Haskell" or "Alice Lillian Hathaway Haskell".[6][10] Her husband's Harvard reports, written during her lifetime, give "Alice Hathaway Haskell" only.[2][13]
* **The profile page's account of her birth and her mother's widowhood.** The profile page says she was born in Hong Kong "because her father ... was working there as a merchant (likely involved in the China trade)", and that her father died "sometime before 1899". *(Unsound: drawn from an AI-generated summary, with no source.)*[1] Her father's profile page gives his death as 25 June 1868.[6]
* **Her mother's dates.** Geni and the profile page give Elizabeth Russell's birth as December 1835 at "Kingston?, England" and her death as 19 December 1909 at Marblehead.[9][19] One family tree, citing Find a Grave, gives birth on 16 December 1837 at Marblehead, death on 19 September 1909 at Marblehead, and burial at Jamaica Plain.[10] Back Bay Houses says she died in September 1909, which supports the September date.[11]
* **Her husband's date of death.** The profile page gives 28 June 1931; Wikipedia, citing the *New York Times*, gives 29 June 1931.[17][20]
* **Russell Burrage's birth date.** The 1913 Harvard report gives 17 December 1889; one family tree gives 18 December 1889.[10][13]
* **Albert Cameron Burrage Jr's birth year.** The 1913 Harvard report and his profile page give 1886; Geni gives 30 August 1887.[9][13]
* **Number of children.** Wikipedia names three children in one place and refers to "their four children" in another. The Harvard report lists four, one of whom, Francis, died in 1911.[13][17]
