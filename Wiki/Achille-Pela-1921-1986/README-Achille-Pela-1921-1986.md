# Achille Pelà (1921–1986)

**Achille Pelà** (7 November 1921 – 1986) was an Italian translator, born in Milan. In 1955 he married Olga Sergeyevna Kudasheva, the only child of Prince [Sergei Vladimirovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933/README-Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933.md) and his second wife, [Olga Erikovna von Pistohlkors](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963/README-Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963.md). He was the father of [Sergio Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergio-Pela-1957-/README-Sergio-Pela-1957-.md).[1][2][3] Almost nothing else about him is recorded here.

## Life

Geni gives his birth in Milan on 7 November 1921.[1] My notes give his dates as 1921–1986.[2]

In 1955 he married Olga Sergeyevna Kudasheva, then 28 or 29. Geni places the marriage in the United States, and has a note that she was "the secretary of the American Consul in Rome".[1] Their three children were born between 1956 and 1964.[1]

My notes record him, with a Rosanna Pelà, as the translator of Eddy Bauer's *Histoire controversée de la deuxième guerre mondiale*, published in Italian by the Istituto Geografico De Agostini in 1976.[2] That hasn't been checked against the book. Another note calls him an Italian historian and translator, but it comes from an AI-drafted text, not a primary source.[4] How Rosanna Pelà was related to him isn't recorded.
