## Alternate Names, Spellings & Locations

* **Modern transliteration:** `Nikolay (or Nikolai) Danilovich Kudashev` (explicitly noting the alternate transliteration `Nikolai` alongside `Nikolay`).

## Discrepancies vs. README

* **Year of promotion to major general:** `Nikolay-Danilovich-Kudashev-1784-1813.md` states: "Promoted to major general and awarded the Order of St. George (3rd class) in 1813". `README-Nikolay-Danilovich-Kudashev-1784-1813.md` dates his promotion to major general to 26 December 1812.
* **Location of mortal wound:** `Nikolay-Danilovich-Kudashev-1784-1813.md` records under Genealogy: `Death: Nov 9 1813 ~ of wounds received at the Battle of Leipzig`, and under Life: `he was mortally wounded at the Battle of Leipzig — the "Battle of the Nations" — and died on November 9, 1813.` `README-Nikolay-Danilovich-Kudashev-1784-1813.md` places the mortal wound near Altenburg in the fighting preceding the Battle of Leipzig (with his death near Leipzig).
