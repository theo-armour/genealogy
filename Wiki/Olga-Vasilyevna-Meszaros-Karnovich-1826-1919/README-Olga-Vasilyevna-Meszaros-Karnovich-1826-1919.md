# Olga Vasilyevna Karnovich, born Meszaros (1826–1919) ~ Ольга Васильевна Карнович, урождённая Месарош

No portrait of her is held in this folder. A photograph of a portrait of her is in the State A. S. Pushkin Museum in Moscow (see Further research).

**Olga Vasilyevna Karnovich**, born **Meszaros** (Russian: Ольга Васильевна Карнович, урождённая Месарош; 1826 or 1830 – August 1919), was the wife of the chamberlain [Valerian Gavrilovich Karnovich](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Valerian-Gavrilovich-Karnovich-1823-1891/README-Valerian-Gavrilovich-Karnovich-1823-1891.md) and the mother of [Princess Olga Paley](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Olga-Valerianovna-Paley-1865-1929/README-Olga-Valerianovna-Paley-1865-1929.md). Her daughter's page describes her as of Hungarian and German descent.[1][2] She was a great-great-grandmother of [Sergio Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergio-Pela-1957-/README-Sergio-Pela-1957-.md).

## Life

Her father, Vasily Meszaros, bore a Hungarian surname. Her mother was Ulyana Yegorovna Paley-Gurkovskaya.[1] Geni gives her birth in Hungary in 1826. The Russian Genealogical Foundation and a museum catalogue give her dates as 1830–1919.[1]

She married Valerian Gavrilovich Karnovich. Their first son, Vladimir, was born at the end of 1845. The Moscow confession register for 1848 lists her as 21, with her husband and two sons.[1] At least seven children followed. The youngest, Olga, was born in St. Petersburg in December 1865 and became Princess Paley.[1][2]

She was widowed in 1891. She lived to see her daughter's marriage to Grand Duke Paul Alexandrovich in 1902, and outlived both her grandson Vladimir Paley, killed at Alapayevsk in 1918, and the Grand Duke, shot in Petrograd on 30 January 1919. She died in August 1919, six months after her daughter escaped to Finland.[2]

The surname Paley, which Nicholas II granted to her daughter in 1915, matches her mother's name, Paley-Gurkovskaya. Whether it was chosen for that reason isn't recorded here.

## Open questions

* **Her birth year.** Geni says 1826. Other sources quoted on Geni say 1830. The 1848 confession register's age of 21 points to about 1827.[1]
* **Born in Hungary?** Geni says so. No record has been seen.
* **The name Paley.** Was her daughter's title of Princess Paley taken from her mother's family, the Paley-Gurkovskys? A question to check against the Paley literature.
* **Where she died.** Not recorded. Her daughter had left Petrograd for Finland about 9 February 1919. Did her mother stay behind?[2]
