# Barbara Allison Armour (1826–1898)

🌳 [See in the family tree](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Barbara-Allison-Armour-1826-1898)

No portrait of her is held in this folder.

**Barbara Allison Armour** (May 1826 – February 1898) was born in Scotland and was the wife of the Chicago grain merchant [George Armour](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/George-Armour-1812-1881/README-George-Armour-1812-1881.md). After his death she completed the tower of the Second Presbyterian Church of Chicago in his memory. She was the mother of [George Allison Armour](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936/README-George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936.md) and of the yachtsman [Allison Vincent Armour](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Allison-Vincent-Armour-1863-1941/README-Allison-Vincent-Armour-1863-1941.md), and Theo Armour's great-great-grandmother.[1][2][3]

## Life

She was born in Scotland in May 1826. The Yale obituary of her son Allison calls her the "daughter of William Allison of Millbank, Scotland, and Chicago".[1][2] Geni names her mother as Barbara Wark.[5]

She married George Armour in Illinois in August 1850. He was a Campbeltown man who had come to Illinois in the 1830s and became a partner in the Chicago grain-elevator firms.[1] Five children are named in the family trees, and the eldest, John William, died in 1865 at the age of thirteen.[1] The Wikipedia article on her husband says that Lawrence Alma-Tadema painted her portrait in London in 1865.[1]

Her husband died at Brighton, England, in June 1881. In 1884 his widow had Norman Williams complete the tower and spire of the Second Presbyterian Church of Chicago in his memory. Her tablet in the south entrance reads, in part: "This tower has been completed to the glory of God and in loving memory of George Armour by his widow A.D. MDCCCLXXXIV."[1]

The Wikipedia article on her husband says that Allison Road in Princeton, where her son George Allison Armour lived from 1895, is named for her.[3]

She died in February 1898. Daniel Harvey's history of the Argyle Settlement says that "Mrs. Armour died on their son's yacht in Cuban waters". Two family trees give the death as 1 February 1898 at Charleston, South Carolina, citing Charleston death records.[1] The *Chicago Tribune* reported "Grieve for Mrs Armour" on 3 February 1898.[1]

## Open questions

* **Birthplace.** One tree gives "Milton, East Lothian, Lanarkshire", which is not a consistent place name. The Wikipedia article says Aberdeen. The Yale record connects her father with Millbank.[1]
* **Date and place of death.** Geni and MyHeritage give February 1893 in Illinois.[5] The *Tribune* of 3 February 1898, the Charleston death records cited by two trees, and her husband's and sons' pages in this repository all give 1898.[1][2][3] Did she die at Charleston, on Allison's yacht in Cuban waters, or both, with the yacht at Charleston?[1]
* **The Alma-Tadema portrait.** Where is it now?
