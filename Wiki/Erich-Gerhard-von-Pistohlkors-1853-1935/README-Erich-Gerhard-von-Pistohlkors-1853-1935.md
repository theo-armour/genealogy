**Erich Gerhard von Pistohlkors** (Russian: Эрик Августович фон Пистолькорс; 25 November 1853 – 8 November 1935) was a Russian officer from a Baltic-German noble family. He served as an aide-de-camp of Grand Duke Vladimir Alexandrovich and reached the rank of major general.[1][2][3] In 1884 he married Olga Valerianovna Karnovich, who left him for Grand Duke Paul Alexandrovich and became [Princess Paley](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Olga-Valerianovna-Paley-1865-1929/README-Olga-Valerianovna-Paley-1865-1929.md). Their daughter [Olga Erikovna](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963/README-Olga-Ericovna-von-Pistohlkors-1888-1963.md) became the second wife of Prince [Sergei Vladimirovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933/README-Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933.md), so he was a great-grandfather of [Sergio Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergio-Pela-1957-/README-Sergio-Pela-1957-.md).[1][2]

## Life

Geni gives his birth at Frankfurt am Main on 25 November 1853. He was the second of six children of August Friedrich Woldemar von Pistohlkors and Emilie Natalie von Harder, who had married in St. Petersburg in 1850.[1] The family were Baltic-German nobility, with land at Koltzen in Livonia.[1]

He became a Guards officer. Princess Paley's page records that he was an aide-de-camp of Grand Duke Vladimir Alexandrovich and later a major general.[2] On 30 May 1884 he married Olga Valerianovna Karnovich in St. Petersburg. They had four children, of whom the first Olga died as an infant.[1][2]

Grand Duke Paul Alexandrovich was a friend of his, and he was a captain in Paul's regiment. Paul spent evenings with the couple at Tsarskoe Selo. In the mid-1890s Paul and Olga began an affair, which became a court scandal.[2] The Pistohlkors marriage ended in divorce, and in 1902 Olga married the Grand Duke in Livorno.[2] Their daughter Olga was thirteen when her parents divorced.[3]

Geni records a second marriage, in 1922, to Maria Golowinski, and gives his death at Riga on 8 November 1935, aged 81.[1]

## Open questions

* **Wladimir.** Geni lists a son Wladimir, born 28 December 1896, under the Pistohlkors marriage. Princess Paley's page names only four children of that marriage, and her son by Grand Duke Paul, Vladimir Paley, was born in 1897.[1][2] Is Geni's Wladimir a confusion with him?
* **The divorce.** Geni gives 12 August 1902, two months before Olga's marriage to the Grand Duke. No record has been seen.
* **The second marriage.** Geni gives St. Petersburg in 1922, which seems unlikely for a former imperial general five years after the revolution. No record has been seen.
* **His career.** The regiments and dates of his service aren't recorded here.
