## Alternate Names, Spellings & Locations

* **Subject:** Also seen as `Feodor Trepov`; `"Trepov senior" ~ to distinguish him from his son Fyodor Fyodorovich Jr.`
* **Spouse:** Spelled `Vera Vasilyevna Lukasevich` (the README spells her surname `Lukashevich`).
* **Daughter Anastasia:** Listed as `Anastasia Fyodorovna (also recorded as Anastatisya Fyodorovna)` and `Anastasia Fyodorovna Nieroth (also recorded as Anastatisya Fyodorovna; née Trepova)` (the README does not mention the variant `Anastatisya`).

## Discrepancies vs. README

* **Alexander Trepov's tenure as Prime Minister:** Describes son **Alexander Trepov** as "Prime Minister of the Russian Empire from 1916 to 1917", whereas the README states "Prime Minister in 1916".
* **Spouse's surname spelling:** `Vera Vasilyevna Lukasevich` vs. `Vera Vasilyevna Lukashevich` in the README.
