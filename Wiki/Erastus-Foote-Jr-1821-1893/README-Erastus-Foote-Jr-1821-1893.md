# Erastus Foote Jr (1821–1893)

🌳 [See in the family tree](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Erastus-Foote-Jr-1821-1893)

No portrait of him is held in this folder.

**Erastus Foote Jr** (6 September 1821 – 20 February 1893) was a lawyer of Wiscasset, Maine, who moved his family to Chicago in 1868. He was the son of [Erastus Foote](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Erastus-Foote-1777-1856/README-Erastus-Foote-1777-1856.md), Maine's first attorney general. His daughter [Harriette Cobb Foote](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Harriette-Cobb-Foote-Armour-1859-1924/README-Harriette-Cobb-Foote-Armour-1859-1924.md) married [George Allison Armour](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936/README-George-Allison-Armour-1856-1936.md).[1][2] He was Theo Armour's great-great-grandfather.

## Life

He was born at Wiscasset on 6 September 1821, the eldest child of Erastus Foote by his second wife, Eliza Carlton. She was the sister of his first wife, Susan.[1] Of his father's seven children he was the only son to survive childhood.[1]

Nathaniel Goodwin's Foote genealogy (1849) says that he "graduated at Bowdoin College" and was a lawyer at Wiscasset in 1849.[1] He married Sarah Page Wood. One family tree names a son Erastus, born in Maine about 1850.[4] Their youngest child, Harriette Cobb, was born on 15 November 1859.[2]

*Foote Family* (1907) says that he moved his family to Chicago in 1868.[2] In April 1877 he and George Armour were ordained elders of the Second Presbyterian Church of Chicago on the same occasion. Five years later his daughter Harriette married George Armour's son George Allison Armour.[2]

He died at Chicago on 20 February 1893, according to the family trees.[3][4]

## Open questions

* **His college.** Goodwin says Bowdoin. The digitised text of William Willis's history of the Maine bar (1863) says he was "a graduate of Harvard in the class of 18-13", presumably 1843.[1]
* **His children.** *Foote Family* calls Harriette the youngest child. The other children are not named in the sources read, except for the son Erastus in one tree.[2][4]
* **The move to Chicago.** Why did he leave Wiscasset in 1868? His father's page asks the same question.[1]
