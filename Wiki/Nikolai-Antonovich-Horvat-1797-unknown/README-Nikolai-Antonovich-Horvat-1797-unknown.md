## Life

Almost nothing is known of him. Geni gives his birth and baptism on 26 May 1797. His parents were Anton Samuilovich Horvat, who died about 1809, and Yekaterina Ivanovna, who had married at Mironovka in Yekaterinoslav Governorate in February 1796.[2]

On 28 April 1821, at Revovka in the same governorate, he married Lubov Alexandrovna Pishchevich. Geni lists children born between 1826 and 1840. At least two, Leonid and Viktor, were born in Odessa. His wife died in Odessa in 1849.[2] Olga, the youngest child recorded, was born in 1840 and married Prince Vladimir Sergeyevich Kudashev.[1]

The Horvat name is shared with the Serbian family of General Ivan Horvat, founder of New Serbia in what became Yelisavetgrad Uyezd. His daughter's page records that no link between that family and this one has been established.[1]

## Open questions

* **Was he Olga's father?** Geni and the unsourced Wikipedia template say so. No record naming her parents has been seen.[1]
* **His death.** Not recorded. Was he alive when his daughter married?
* **The Serbian Horvats.** Was he a descendant of General Ivan Horvat? His father's patronymic, Samuilovich, could be checked against the general's family.
* **His career.** Nothing is recorded.
