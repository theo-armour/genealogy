# Elizabeth Russell Haskell

🌳 [See in the GEDCOM Viewer](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Elizabeth-Russell-1835-1909)

**Elizabeth Russell** (December 1835 or 1837 – 1909), known after her marriage as Mrs Francis H. Haskell, was the wife of the Massachusetts shipmaster [Francis Hathaway Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868/README-Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868.md). Most sources say she was born in England. She was married by 1857, lived for about ten years at Canton and Hong Kong, where her children were born, and spent her long widowhood in Roxbury and Boston, Massachusetts.[1][2][3][4] Her dates of birth and death are both in conflict (see Open questions). She was the mother of [Alice Hathaway Haskell Burrage](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947/README-Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947.md) and Theo Armour's maternal great-great-grandmother.[5]

## Family and early life

Very little about her early life is established. Geni and her profile page give her birth as December 1835 at "Kingston?, England", with the question mark, and name her father as Robert Russell.[1][6] One Ancestry-derived tree gives 16 December 1837 at Kingston, Surrey, England; another gives the same day at Marblehead, Massachusetts.[7][8] No birth or baptism record has been seen.

She had a brother, William F. Russell, who was living with her children in Boston in 1910 and was a bachelor.[4]

She probably also had a sister in China. Edward Kenneth Haviland, writing on American shipowners there, says of her husband and Captain James Bridges Endicott that "it seems that Mrs. Endicott and Mrs. Haskell were sisters".[3] A biographical note on Endicott at Gwulo, a Hong Kong history site, says that he married an Englishwoman, Sarah Ann Russell, in 1852, and that she was "a daughter of Robert and Sarah Anne (Cork) Russell, of Brixton, County of Surrey, England".[9] The father's name agrees with Geni's for Elizabeth, and Elizabeth named a daughter Sarah Anne.[6] If Haviland is right, her mother was Sarah Anne Cork. None of this has been checked against an English record.

## Marriage

She married Francis Hathaway Haskell, a son of [Elisha Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Elisha-Haskell-Jr-1795-1880/README-Elisha-Haskell-Jr-1795-1880.md) of Boston and New Bedford. The *Chronicles of the Haskell Family* (1943) enters him as "Francis H. m Elizabeth Russell", and her son-in-law named "Francis Hathaway Haskell and Elizabeth (Russell) Haskell" as his wife's parents in his Harvard class report.[10][11] No record of the marriage has been found, and its date and place are not known.

It had taken place by late 1857. Her husband was then master of the Boston ship *Norseman*, which left Macao and Hong Kong in November 1857 and reached New York in March 1858. The *New York Times* listed among the passengers "In ship Norseman, from Macao—Mrs. Henry Carrol and child, Mrs. F. H. Haskell".[2]

## Canton and Hong Kong

By 1861 her husband was a partner in Thomas Hunt & Co., shipwrights at Whampoa, below Canton, and from about 1864 he commanded the river steamer *Kinshan* between Hong Kong and Canton; see his biography.[3][12]

The family's years in China are marked by two newspaper notices. The *London and China Telegraph* of 10 June 1865 printed the death, in April, "at Canton, very suddenly, of cholera", of "Bessie, daughter of [Franc]is H. and Elizabeth R. Haskell, aged five years and six months".[13] The same paper on 28 August 1867 printed a birth:

> On the 9th July, at Hong Kong, the wife of Capt. F. H. Haskell, of a daughter.[14]

Her children, as far as the sources read show them, were:

* **Elizabeth, called Bessie**, born about the autumn of 1859; died at Canton in April 1865.[13] Geni lists an Elizabeth Hathaway Haskell without dates, probably the same child.[6]
* **[Alice Hathaway Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947/README-Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947.md)**, born on 6 October in 1857, 1861 or 1862, in Hong Kong or "China"; died in 1947.[5]
* **Francis Hathaway Haskell Jr**, born at Hong Kong on 19 July 1863 and died at Brookline, Massachusetts, on 1 March 1927, according to Geni.[6] *Back Bay Houses* says he died in March 1927.[5]
* **Robert Hathaway Haskell**, born at Hong Kong and died in China, according to Geni, which gives no dates.[6]
* **Sarah Anne Haskell**, born at Hong Kong, according to Geni; probably the daughter born on 9 July 1867.[6][14]

Two family trees say that her husband was lost overboard on 25 June 1868 on a voyage from Hong Kong to New York. No contemporary record of his death has been found.[8][12]

## Roxbury and Boston

When she returned to Massachusetts is not known. In November 1885 her daughter Alice was married at St. James Church, Roxbury, and Alice's husband described her parents as "of Roxbury, Mass."[11] According to Alice's biography in this repository, which cites *Back Bay Houses*, Mrs Haskell lived at The Warren, at Warren and Regent Streets in Roxbury, before she moved to the Back Bay.[5]

On 24 June 1899 "Elizabeth (Russell) Haskell, the widow of Francis Hathaway Haskell", bought the house at 7 Gloucester Street, Boston, from James Barr Ames. Her daughter Sarah A. Haskell lived with her. After buying the house she had a two-story addition built at the rear.[4] Her daughter Alice Burrage had bought the land for the Burrage house at 314 Commonwealth Avenue in January of the same year.[5]

## Death

*Back Bay Houses* says that "Elizabeth Haskell died in September of 1909 and 7 Gloucester was inherited by Sarah Haskell".[4] Two family trees give the date as 19 September 1909, at Marblehead; one of them cites Find a Grave and gives her burial place as Jamaica Plain.[7][8] Geni and her profile page give 19 December 1909, at Marblehead.[1][6] No death record or obituary has been seen.

In 1910 Sarah Haskell was still at 7 Gloucester Street, with her brother Francis and their uncle William F. Russell. By 1911 they had all moved, Sarah and Francis to Brookline. Sarah kept the house and let it.[4]

## Residences

* **England** (Kingston or Brixton, Surrey), if the sources that give an English birth are right.[6][7][9]
* **Macao, Whampoa, Canton and Hong Kong**, from 1857 or earlier until about 1868. She sailed from Macao in November 1857; a daughter died at Canton in 1865 and another was born at Hong Kong in 1867.[2][13][14]
* **Roxbury, Massachusetts**, by 1885; later at The Warren, Warren and Regent Streets.[5][11]
* **7 Gloucester Street, Boston**, from June 1899 until her death in 1909.[4]
* **Marblehead, Massachusetts**, where the family trees say she died.[6][7][8]

## Open questions

* **Date of birth.** December 1835 (Geni, the profile page, the folder name) or 16 December 1837 (two Ancestry-derived trees, one citing Find a Grave).[1][6][7][8]
* **Place of birth.** "Kingston?, England" (Geni), Kingston, Surrey (one tree) or Marblehead, Massachusetts (another tree).[6][7][8] Marblehead is also given as her place of death, and the tree that has her born there may have copied it across. If she was Mrs Endicott's sister, the family was of Brixton, Surrey.[9]
* **Date of death.** *Back Bay Houses* says September 1909, and two trees say 19 September.[4][7][8] Geni and the profile page say 19 December.[1][6] September has the better support; "December" may have been carried over from the month of her birth.
* **Her parents.** Geni names only her father, Robert Russell, with the note "Descended from Irish Lord Russell". *(Unsound: no source.)*[6] Her mother is named nowhere in the repository. Sarah Anne Cork depends on Haviland's "it seems" and on an unsourced note at Gwulo.[3][9]
* **Whether she was Mrs Endicott's sister.** Haviland's wording is cautious, and his authority appears to be a private letter of 1862 that has not been seen.[3]
* **Date and place of marriage.** Geni says "about 1860".[6] She was already Mrs F. H. Haskell in November 1857.[2] Whether she married in England, in China or in America is not known.
* **Her middle name.** The 1865 notice calls her Elizabeth R. Haskell, and the address of the Find a Grave memorial cited by one tree ends "elizabeth-r.-haskell".[8][13] One Geni export and two family trees give her middle name as "Llnnnrll", which is not a name and looks like a damaged entry.[7][8][15]
* **An 1865 Massachusetts census entry.** One tree attaches to her an entry from the Massachusetts state census of 1865 for a single woman living in Boston.[8] In 1865 she was married and living in China, so this entry probably belongs to another Elizabeth Russell.[13]
* **Which daughter was born in 1867.** The notice does not name the child. Sarah Anne is assumed.[14]
* **The profile pages on her widowhood.** Her husband's profile page says she lived at 7 Gloucester Street "with her daughter's family". *(Unsound: from an AI-generated summary.)* *Back Bay Houses* shows that it was her unmarried daughter Sarah who lived with her; the Burrages had their own house.[4][5]
