# Princess Mathilde Kudasheva, born Countess de Choiseul-Gouffier (1806–1867) ~ Матильда Октавьевна Кудашева

🌳 [See in the family tree](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Mathilde-de-Choiseul-Gouffier-Kudasheva-1806-1867)

No portrait of her is held in this folder. A portrait of her grandfather is on her husband's page.

**Princess Mathilde Kudasheva**, born **Countess Mathilde de Choiseul-Gouffier** (Russian: Матильда Октавьевна Кудашева, née графиня Шуазель-Гуфье; 1806 – 1867), was the wife of Prince [Sergei Danilovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Danilovich-Kudashev-1795-1862/README-Sergei-Danilovich-Kudashev-1795-1862.md), Vice-Governor of Kiev. They had ten children.[1][2] Her grandson was the art collector Prince [Sergei Vladimirovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933/README-Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933.md), so she was my great-great-great-grandmother, and a great-great-grandmother of [Sergio Pelà](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergio-Pela-1957-/README-Sergio-Pela-1957-.md). Through her daughter Alexandra she was also a grandmother of the philosopher Nikolai Berdyaev.[1]

## Life

Geni gives her baptismal record: Vilnius, 19 March 1806.[2] Her husband's page explains her family. Her patronymic, Oktavyevna, points to Count Antoine-Louis-Octave de Choiseul-Gouffier. He came to Russia with his father, served as chamberlain to the Russian emperor, and married Countess Wiktoria Potocka in 1801. His father, Count Marie-Gabriel-Florent-Auguste de Choiseul-Gouffier (1752–1817), had been French ambassador to the Ottoman Empire. Paul I made him director of the Imperial Academy of Arts and of the Imperial Public Library.[1]

She married Prince Sergei Danilovich Kudashev and lived with him in Kiev. Count Mikhail Buturlin, who was in Kiev in 1836, remembered "another hospitable house": that of Prince Kudashev, "married to Countess Mathilde Choiseul-Gouffier".[1] Her husband was Vice-Governor of Kiev from 1838 to 1844, and then marshal of the nobility of Kherson Governorate.[1]

They had ten children, named in the Senate's list of titled families and in a patent of nobility of about 1864.[1] Among them were Alexander, a Kiev banker; Vladimir, my great-great-grandfather; Sergei, a Moscow banker and historian; and Alexandra, the mother of Nikolai Berdyaev.[1] Berdyaev wrote about his Kudashev and Choiseul-Gouffier ancestry in his autobiography, *Самопознание* (*Self-Knowledge*).[1]

She was widowed in March 1862 and died in 1867.[1][2]

## Open questions

* **Her parents.** Her husband's page infers her father from her patronymic. Geni names him and her mother outright, from a family memoir by a descendant. No record naming them has been seen here.[1][2]
* **Her birth.** Vilnius, 19 March 1806, from a baptismal record on Geni. The Praslin genealogy gives "1805/6".[2]
* **Her death.** The year 1867 is agreed. Only one genealogy gives the month, October, and none gives the place.[2]
