# Olga Nikolaevna Kudasheva (1840–1907) ~ Ольга Николаевна Кудашева

No portrait of her is held in this folder.

**Princess Olga Nikolaevna Kudasheva**, born **Horvat** (Russian: Ольга Николаевна Кудашева, née Хорват; 15 April 1840 – 25 October 1907), was the wife of [Prince Vladimir Sergeyevich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Vladimir-Sergeyevich-Kudashev-1833-1871/README-Vladimir-Sergeyevich-Kudashev-1833-1871.md). She was widowed in 1871, at the age of 31, outlived him by 36 years and died at Yalta. Her son was the art collector [Prince Sergei Vladimirovich Kudashev](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933/README-Sergei-Vladimirovich-Kudashev-1863-1933.md), and she was Theo Armour's great-great-grandmother.[1][2]

## Life

The pages for her husband and son give her birth in 1840. Geneanet gives the date as 15 April.[1] She married Prince Vladimir Sergeyevich Kudashev, a lieutenant and zemstvo deputy in Yelisavetgrad Uyezd. The genealogical forum VGD lists five children, the eldest, Matilda, born in 1857. That date would put the marriage at about 1856, when she was about sixteen. It has not been checked against a primary record.[1]

Her son Sergei was born in Kiev in 1863. Her husband died in Kiev in 1871, leaving her a widow with young children.[1][3] Her grandson Nikolai Vsevolodovich Kudashev (1903–1978), the son of Vsevolod, was born in Kremenchuk and died in New York, and is described as a poet.[1]

She died at Yalta on 25 October 1907.[1][4]

## Open questions

* **Birthplace.** Her husband's page and Geneanet say Kiev.[1] Geni says Odessa, and links a baptism register of the Pokrovskaya (Intercession) church in Odessa for 1840 on FamilySearch.[4] The register has not been read for this page.
* **Her parents.** The family-tree template on the Russian Wikipedia article about her grandson Nikolai gives her father as Nikolai Antonovich Horvat, born 1797. It cites no source.[1] Geni gives the same father, born 26 May 1797, and a mother, Lubov Alexandrovna Pishchevich, married 28 April 1821.[4] No record naming her parents has been seen.
* **The Horvat family.** The surname is shared with the Serbian family of General Ivan Horvat, founder of New Serbia in what became Yelisavetgrad Uyezd. No link between that family and Olga Nikolaevna has been established.[1]
