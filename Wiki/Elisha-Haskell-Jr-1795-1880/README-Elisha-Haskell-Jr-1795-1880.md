# Elisha Haskell

🌳 [See in the family tree](https://theo-armour.github.io/gedcom-viewer/#wiki=Elisha-Haskell-Jr-1795-1880)

**Elisha Haskell** (about 1798 – 8 October 1880) was a Boston auctioneer with family roots in Middleborough and New Bedford, Massachusetts. For about twenty years he was a partner in the Boston auction house of Coolidge & Haskell. His second wife, Alice Russell Hathaway, was a daughter of the New Bedford merchant Humphrey Hathaway, and in later life the couple lived in her father's house at the Head of the River in Acushnet, where his funeral was held.[1][2][3][4] Through his daughter Caroline he was the grandfather of Alice Hathaway Lee, the first wife of Theodore Roosevelt.[4][5] His year of birth is uncertain (see Open questions). Through his son [Francis Hathaway Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868/README-Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868.md) and his granddaughter [Alice Hathaway Haskell Burrage](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947/README-Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947.md) he was Theo Armour's maternal great-great-great-grandfather.[6][7]

## Family and early life

Elisha Haskell was a son of Eli Haskell and Achsah Nye.[7][8] His father was born at Middleborough on 12 May 1775, the son of Lt. Elisha Haskell and his wife Anna.[9] The Middleborough records enter "Mr. Eli Haskell of Middleborough & Miss Achsa Nye, of Oakham" under 16 September 1797, and the Oakham records give the intention of "Eli of Midleborough and Achse Nye" on 8 October 1797.[9][10]

The *Chronicles of the Haskell Family* (1943) says that the elder Elisha, who married Anna King in 1767, lived near "Hay Hall" in Middleborough and later moved "to Acushnet and then New Bedford", and that Eli, although living in Oakham when he married, died in New Bedford, "where his children continued". Of the subject it says only: "His son, Elisha (7) lived in New Bedford, 'at the head of the river.'"[8]

The family trees give his birthplace as Middleborough. They do not agree on the year (see Open questions), and no record of his birth was found in the published Middleborough or Oakham records searched for this biography.[7][9][10][11][12]

## New Bedford and first marriage

"Elisha and Sally [intention: Sarah] Tobey, both of N. B.", were married at New Bedford on 17 October 1820.[13] The New Bedford records enter the death of an unnamed wife of Elisha Haskell on 10 April 1823, from the printed diary of Joseph R. Anthony.[13] Geni names her Sarah Tobey "Sally" Haskell and gives the same date.[7]

Their son George Lewis Haskell was living in his father's house in Boston in 1845, when the directory lists him as a member of the firm of Huston, Robbins & Co.[3] He died at Singapore on 1 May 1860, of erysipelas, aged 37; the notice calls him "George Lewis Haskell, Esq., of Boston, Mass."[14]

Daniel Ricketson, recalling Water Street in New Bedford "about 1823", named Elisha Haskell among the young men "in the 'world's' costume", as distinct from the dress of Friends, who were then active in business there.[15] An Elisha Haskell is also named in the charter of the Merchants Bank of New Bedford, and an Elisha Haskell and an Eli Haskell in the charter of the Merchants Insurance Company, which was incorporated at the same time.[16] The bank's history does not say which Elisha is meant.

## Boston auctioneer

By 1826 he was in Boston. The directory of that year lists "Haskell Elisha, see D. & P. Thacher & Co.", and that of 1828 gives his house as High Street.[3] When his intention to marry Alice R. Hathaway was published at New Bedford on 1 July 1826, he was "Elisha of Boston".[13]

From 1830 the Boston directories list the firm of "Coolidge (Samuel F.) & Haskell (Elisha), auctioneers", at 67 and 69 Kilby Street. By 1845 the firm was at 29 and 31 Water Street, and it was still listed in the directory for 1848–1849.[2] In the directories of 1853 and 1858 he appears with a house address only.[3]

In 1856 the new ship *Norseman*, of Boston, was registered with his son Francis H. Haskell as master. Four fifths of her belonged to the copartners Arthur Cunningham, John A. Cunningham and C. Loring Cunningham, and one fifth to "Elisha H. Haskell", of Boston.[17] The owner is probably the subject, since the master was his son and, according to Geni, his daughter Alice had married John Adams Cunningham Jr in 1850; but no other source read gives him a middle initial.[7][17]

## Second marriage and children

Alice Russell Hathaway was a daughter of Humphrey Hathaway (1765–1821) of New Bedford and his wife Abigail Smith. *Representative Men and Old Families of Southeastern Massachusetts* lists her among Humphrey's children as "Alice, born in 1806, married in 1826 Elisha Haskell, and died in 1880", and says that her brothers Francis S. and Thomas S. Hathaway "were rich merchants of New Bedford".[18]

The *Chronicles of the Haskell Family* gives Elisha six children by Alice Hathaway.[8] With dates from Geni, which cites no sources for them, they were:[7][8]

* **Alice Hathaway Haskell** (1829–1908), who married a Cunningham. Geni names him John Adams Cunningham Jr and dates the marriage 4 June 1850 at Boston.
* **[Francis Hathaway Haskell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868/README-Francis-Hathaway-Haskell-1830-1868.md)**, who married Elizabeth Russell. He was master of the ship *Norseman* and later a steamer captain at Hong Kong.[17]
* **Caroline Watts Haskell** (1835–1914). She married George Cabot Lee at Boston on 10 December 1857; his Harvard class report calls her "dau. of Elisha Haskell and Alice Russell Hathaway".[5]
* **Thomas Hathaway Haskell** (1837–1893). In 1858 the Boston directory lists Thomas H. Haskell, clerk, at his father's address.[3]
* **Frederick E. Haskell** (1843–1909); Geni gives his middle name as Elihu.
* **Charlotte Haskell** (1845–1897). The New Bedford records, from a gravestone in the Rural Cemetery, call her "Charlotte H., w. John L. Bullard, d. Elisha and Alice", born in 1845.[13]

A book on old Boston sea captains, published in 1918, says that "Frank Haskell, who commanded the 'Norseman,' was an elder brother of Thomas and Frederick Haskell".[19]

The Lees' second daughter, Alice Hathaway Lee, born 29 July 1861, married Theodore Roosevelt and died on 14 February 1884.[5]

## Later years and death

One family tree records him in New Bedford in 1865.[12] The house at the Head of the River in Acushnet, near the post office, had been built by Humphrey Hathaway after he bought the land from the Blackwell heirs, and it descended to his daughter Alice. Franklyn Howland's *History of the Town of Acushnet* (1907) says that the property "remained in the possession of the Haskell family until 1881".[4]

Alice died on 17 April 1880. A Boston weekly printed the notice: "In New Bedford, April 17th, Alice R., wife of Elisha Haskell, 74 years 1 month 17 days."[20]

Elisha Haskell died on 8 October 1880, according to Geni and three family trees, all of which give the place as New Bedford.[7][11][12] Howland wrote of the funeral:

> Miss Alice Lee, who was the first wife of Theodore Roosevelt, attended the funeral of her grandfather, Elisha Haskell, which was held in the old Hathaway–Haskell house, and Alice Roosevelt's father, President Roosevelt, came with her.[4]

No death record, obituary or burial record has been seen.

## Residences

* **Middleborough, Massachusetts**, his birthplace according to the family trees.[7][11][12]
* **New Bedford, Massachusetts.** "Of N. B." at his first marriage in 1820, and recalled there about 1823.[13][15]
* **Boston, Massachusetts**, by 1826. High Street in 1828; 2 Tremont Place from 1830 to at least 1845; 12 Ashburton in 1848–1849; 8 Mount Vernon in 1853; 14 Mount Vernon in 1858.[3] One family tree records him in Boston, ward 4, in 1850.[12]
* **New Bedford and Acushnet, Massachusetts.** In New Bedford in 1865, according to one family tree.[12] The Hathaway–Haskell house at the Head of the River, Acushnet, where his funeral was held in 1880.[4][8]

## Open questions

* **Year of birth.** Geni and the profile page give **1795**.[1][7] Two Ancestry-derived trees give **1798**.[11] The MyHeritage export gives **1802**.[12] His parents' marriage was entered at Middleborough and Oakham in the autumn of 1797, so 1795 is unlikely.[9][10] The name of this folder uses 1795. No birth or death record giving his age has been seen.
* **"Jr."** The folder name and the profile page call him Elisha Haskell Jr.[1] None of the records read for this biography uses "Jr." for him. His father was Eli; the elder Elisha was his grandfather.[8][9]
* **Date of the second marriage.** The New Bedford records give only the intention, 1 July 1826, and *Representative Men* says "married in 1826".[13][18] Geni gives 27 July 1827 at New Bedford.[7] The marriage record itself has not been seen.
* **Alice Hathaway's birth.** Two trees give July 1806 at Acushnet; Geni gives 1806 at New Bedford.[7][11] Her age at death as printed in 1880, 74 years 1 month 17 days, works out to a birth at about the beginning of March 1806.[20]
* **Place of death.** The trees say New Bedford.[7][11][12] The funeral was at the house in Acushnet.[4] Acushnet was a separate town by 1880, so the place of death needs checking against the death record.
* **"Elisha H. Haskell".** The 1856 register of the *Norseman* gives her part-owner a middle initial that appears nowhere else.[17]
* **Two entries in the Boston directory of 1828.** It lists "Haskell Elisha, house High" and "Haskell Elisha, (D. & P. Thacher & Co.) house High". These may be one man entered twice.[3]
* **His daughter Charlotte's name.** The gravestone record gives "Charlotte H." and her husband as John L. Bullard; the *Chronicles* gives "Charlotte G. m Dr. John H. Bullard"; Geni gives Charlotte Green Haskell.[7][8][13]
* **"Elizabeth Haskell", born 1834 in England.** Geni lists a daughter of this name and also separate children "Fred" and "Tom".[7] The *Chronicles* lists six children and no Elizabeth.[8] The entry may be a confusion with his daughter-in-law, [Elizabeth Russell](https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Wiki/Elizabeth-Russell-1835-1909/README-Elizabeth-Russell-1835-1909.md), whom Geni records as born in England in 1835; "Fred" and "Tom" look like duplicates of Frederick and Thomas.
* **The profile page's account of his later career.** The profile page says he was "later associated with the building trade" and may have been in a contracting partnership with Anselm J. Bourne that built houses in Fairhaven. *(Unsound: from an AI-generated summary, with no source; nothing found supports it.)*[1]
* **The 1850 census.** The profile page says the 1850 census lists him in Boston as an auctioneer.[1] The census has not been seen; the Boston directories confirm the occupation.[2]
