# Burrage Read Me

My mother was a Burrage. This folder is about her father's family, the Burrages of Boston, and her father's mother's family, the Haskells. Her mother's family, the Shirks, have their own folder: [Shirk](../Shirk/README-Shirk.md).

## The Burrages

* [George Sanderson Burrage](../Wiki/George-Sanderson-Burrage-1823-1876/README-George-Sanderson-Burrage-1823-1876.md) (1823–1876) ~ my 2x-great-grandfather. Chair-maker at Burrageville, Ashburnham, then a vineyard owner in Napa, California
* [Albert Cameron Burrage Sr](../Wiki/Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Sr-1859-1931/README-Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Sr-1859-1931.md) (1859–1931) ~ my great-grandfather. Lawyer, gas-company president, copper-mining promoter and orchid grower
* [Alice Hathaway Haskell Burrage](../Wiki/Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947/README-Alice-Hathaway-Haskell-Burrage-1861-1947.md) ~ his wife, my great-grandmother. Her year of birth is uncertain
* [Albert Cameron Burrage Jr](../Wiki/Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Jr-1886-1978/README-Albert-Cameron-Burrage-Jr-1886-1978.md) (1886–1978) ~ my grandfather. Copper executive and vegetable gardener
* [Anne Bell Burrage](../Wiki/Anne-Bell-Burrage-1890-1983/README-Anne-Bell-Burrage-1890-1983.md), born Shirk ~ his wife, my grandmother. A founder and first president of The Herb Society of America. Her year of birth is uncertain
* [Albert Cameron Burrage III](../Wiki/Albert-Cameron-Burrage-III-1919-1944/README-Albert-Cameron-Burrage-III-1919-1944.md) (1919–1944) ~ my uncle, known as Cameron. Killed in Brittany in 1944
* [Cynthia Sewall Burrage Hovey](../Wiki/Cynthia-Burrage-Hovey-1921-1983/README-Cynthia-Burrage-Hovey-1921-1983.md) (1921–1983) ~ my mother
