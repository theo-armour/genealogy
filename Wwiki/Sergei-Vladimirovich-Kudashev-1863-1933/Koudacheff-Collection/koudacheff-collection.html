Artists in the Koudacheff Painting Collection supplement

Birth and death dates below follow the supplement unless otherwise noted. Repeated entries are combined. Work details, source citations, and available provenance are transcribed or summarized from the supplement. “Not stated” means the supplement does not give that information. For attributions where the supplement gives no dates, linked references are used where available. RKD links include the RKDartists identifier in the URL.

Work-specific web references are linked beneath entries when a title, provenance, or identifying details support the match. Potential matches are labeled as uncertain; unrelated works with similar titles are not included as identifications.
